Better Auth's [Multi Session plugin](https://better-auth.com/docs/plugins/multi-session): people sign in to more than
one account in the same browser, a work and a personal one for example, and switch between them without signing out.

## Turning it on

```ts [nuxt.config.ts]
export default defineNuxtConfig({
  betterAuthUi: {
    multiSession: { enabled: true }
  }
})
```

No migration needed.

## How people use it

- **Adding an account:** "Add another account" opens the sign-in page, which signed-in people can use for this. It
  says they'll stay signed in to the account they're using. Any way of signing in works, and signing up a new account
  too. Afterwards they're back where they started, using the new account.
- **Switching:** the account page's Devices tab lists the accounts signed in on this device, with **Switch** and
  **Sign out** for each. Switching reloads the page as the other account.
- **Signing out** signs out of every account on the device. To leave just one, use its **Sign out** on the Devices
  tab.
- **The admin's menu** gets a "Switch account" submenu, for staff with a personal account and a staff one.

Signing in to an account that's already on the device replaces its old session instead of adding it twice.

## In your app

`<AuthAccountSwitcher />` shows the account in use, and opens to switch, add an account, or sign out of them all. Put
it in your header or sidebar:

```vue [app/components/AppHeader.vue]
<template>
  <header class="flex items-center justify-between">
    <AppLogo />
    <AuthAccountSwitcher />
  </header>
</template>
```

For your own UI, `useAuthDeviceAccounts()` gives the accounts and the actions:

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
const { accounts, switchTo, signOutOf, addAccountLink } = useAuthDeviceAccounts()
</script>

<template>
  <ul>
    <li v-for="account in accounts" :key="account.token">
      {{ account.user.name }}
      <UButton v-if="!account.current" label="Switch" @click="switchTo(account)" />
    </li>
  </ul>
  <UButton v-if="addAccountLink" label="Add another account" :to="addAccountLink" />
</template>
```

Both show nothing without the plugin.

## Options

::field-group
  ::field{name="enabled" type="boolean" default="false"}
  ::

  ::field{name="maximumSessions" type="number" default="5"}
  Accounts signed in at once, per browser. Signing in to one more doesn't add it.
  ::
::

::note
Guests can't add accounts: signing in from a guest account keeps it instead, as
[guest accounts](/sign-in/methods#guest-accounts) always do.
::
