`@treygrr/nuxt-better-auth-ui` is a Nuxt module that adds the pages around [Better Auth](https://better-auth.com), so
you don't build them yourself:

- **[Sign-in pages](/sign-in/pages):** sign in, sign up, forgot and reset password, two-factor, and the extras you turn
  on: passkeys, sign-in links, codes by email or text, guests, single sign-on.
- **An [account page](/sign-in/account)** where people manage their own profile, security, devices and data.
- **[Organizations](/organizations):** each organization's own page, with members, invitations, teams, roles and SSO
  providers.
- **An [admin dashboard](/admin)** at `/admin`, where staff manage users through Better Auth's
  [Admin plugin](https://better-auth.com/docs/plugins/admin).
- **[Auth emails](/customization/emails)** with your own templates and delivery.

Everything is configured under `betterAuthUi` in `nuxt.config.ts`. When a feature needs a Better Auth plugin, the
module adds it for you, and the pages also pick up plugins your own Better Auth config adds.

## Built on

- [Better Auth](https://better-auth.com) through [`@nuxtjs/better-auth`](https://better-auth.nuxt.dev): every action
  goes through Better Auth, which checks it on its server.
- [Nuxt UI](https://ui.nuxt.com): the pages use its components, so they follow your app's colours and dark mode.
- Nuxt 4.
