`@treygrr/nuxt-better-auth-ui` is a Nuxt module that adds the pages around [Better Auth](https://better-auth.com):

- **Sign-in pages:** sign in, sign up, forgot and reset password, two-factor, and the extras you turn on (passkeys,
  sign-in links, codes by email or text, guests, single sign-on).
- **An account page** where people manage their own profile, security, devices and organizations.
- **Organizations:** each organization's own page, with members, invitations, teams, roles and SSO providers.
- **An admin dashboard** at `/admin`, where staff manage users through Better Auth's
  [Admin plugin](https://better-auth.com/docs/plugins/admin).
- **Auth emails** with your own templates and delivery.

Everything is configured under `betterAuthUi` in `nuxt.config.ts`. When a feature needs a Better Auth plugin, the
module adds it for you, and the pages also pick up plugins your own Better Auth config adds.
